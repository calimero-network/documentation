Two steps, kept apart on purpose:

- **Deploy** makes a version of your package available on a cluster. Nothing of your app runs yet.
- **Create an instance** starts one running copy in one organization. Its `init` runs once, with that instance's init
  arguments, so every organization that uses your app starts from its own state.

## Deploy a version

<Steps>

1. In the console, open **Deploy an app** and pick the package: one of your organization's private packages, or a
   public one from the registry.
2. Name the app and pick the version.
3. Choose where it runs: your organization's **shared** cluster, or one of your **dedicated** clusters. The console
   shows the estimated cost.
4. Press **Deploy**, then confirm with your passkey. Your browser installs the version on the cluster.

</Steps>

Deploying again with the same name is an upgrade, from the app's page.

## Create an instance for an organization

On the app's page, the **Instances** box lists the instances you created and creates new ones. For each one you pick:

| Field | What it decides |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | Where the instance lives and who reaches it. The app's own organization (created with its first instance), a new organization (for example a customer's), or one an earlier instance created. |
| **Instance name** | Its name on the cluster. |
| **Init arguments** | JSON your `#[app::init]` receives: this instance's starting state. For an `init(title: String)`, `{"title": "Globex notes"}`. |

Then confirm with your passkey: your browser creates the organization if it is new, and the instance in it. If the
browser tab closes before the console hears back, running the same step again finds the instance it made rather than
creating a second one.

Your app's own frontend can create instances the same way with mero-js (`createOrg`, then `createApp` with
`initArgs`), for example when a new customer signs up. See [Clients](/clients/).

## Upgrades

An upgrade installs the new version and upgrades every instance in **the app's own organization**, after one passkey
confirmation. An instance in another organization is upgraded by that organization's owner, so a customer decides
when their instance moves. An app with no instance yet only installs the new version.

<Aside type="note">
Upgrades are planned from your two versions' embedded ABIs, run your migration, and refuse a downgrade. See
[What an app sees](/build/app-model/#upgrades).
</Aside>
